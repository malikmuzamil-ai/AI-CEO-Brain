Sell-by-Chat: Embedded in AI CRO

Stop selling. Start serving. The sale is the side effect.

Cold outreach converts at 1.7%. Inbound-led outbound converts at 14.6%, an 8.5× difference. The AI CRO is built around that gap. Every DM sequence, follow-up, objection reframe, and close in the CRO follows the Sell-by-Chat playbook, with character counts, personality calibration, and Volume Tax protection on every output.

LVQ Framework

Lube, Value, Question: the qualifying rhythm applied to every sales conversation. Acknowledge, give, then ask. Never just ask.

A-B Gap Method

Point A: where they are. Point B: where they want to be. Stretch the gap. The bigger it feels, the clearer the urgency for a solution.

5-Touch Follow-Up

80% of sales happen after the 5th touchpoint. Every follow-up adds something new. Never repeats. Day 1, 3, 7, 14 rhythm built in.

02

Monday to Friday Loop

The complete weekly operating rhythm. CEO Brain sets the week. Executives execute their handoffs. Friday closes the loop.

📅

Monday: CEO Brain prep and session

Run /monday-prep reads last week's executive reports, calendar, open pipeline. Say "Monday session" to run the 5-step ritual. Then /write-business-state and /executive-handoff.

⚙

Tuesday: COO and CFO check in

AI COO: /sync-ceo-brain then "COO mode": ops check against this week's priority. AI CFO: same flow. Does the financial position support it?

📣

Wednesday: CMO content and CRO pipeline

AI CMO: /content-map-agent or "CMO mode" for content work. AI CRO: /pipeline-scan-agent to catch anything going cold mid-week.

📈

Thursday: CRO follow-up and proposals

AI CRO: discovery call prep via "prep me for a call with [name]". Proposals via /proposal-builder-agent. Update state after.

✅

Friday: executive reports then CEO check

Each executive runs /weekly-executive-report. Back to CEO Brain for /friday-check: reads Monday's commitment and all executive reports.

03

Apollo Lead Campaign

Build a targeted lead list from Apollo, enrich it with LinkedIn data via Apify, write personalised DM sequences, then run a coordinated content campaign to warm the audience.

🔍

CEO Brain: Build the Apollo lead list

Run /apollo-lead-builder in CEO Brain. Define ICP criteria (title, company size, industry). Apify scrapes Apollo and LinkedIn. Every lead scored 1-10. Top leads flagged with specific personalisation signals.

📈

AI CRO: Build personalised sequences for top leads

Run /cold-outreach-sequencer in AI CRO. It loads the lead list from Drive, builds a 5-touch DM sequence per lead using their LinkedIn post data. Message 1 references something specific they wrote.

🎯

CEO Brain: Launch a coordinated campaign

Run /campaign-runner. Define campaign goal, duration, and offer. Campaign runner writes the content sequence for CMO and the DM activation sequence for CRO. Both departments get handoff directives.

📣

AI CMO: Write all campaign post copy

Run /linkedin-campaign-content in AI CMO. Reads the campaign plan from Notion. Writes every post fully: hook, body, CTA, pinned comment. Ready to schedule.

💸

AI CRO: Follow up as leads engage

As DMs come in from the keyword CTA, CRO activates the prospect intelligence brief for each high-signal lead via /prospect-intel-scraper before responding.

Competitor Intelligence

Top 5 content gaps nobody is covering. 3 "only you" post ideas grounded in the founder's unique experience. Recommended niche to own. Saved to Drive and Notion.

📅

AI CMO: Build 30-day calendar from the gaps

Run /content-map-agent with the competitor analysis as context. The 30-day plan is built around the identified gaps, not what competitors are already doing.

09

Quarterly Reset

Start of every quarter. CEO Brain sets the rocks. All 4 executives align to those rocks. Business state fully refreshed. Every department knows the plan.

🎯

CEO Brain: Run /quarterly-plan

Reads current business state, applies constraint-removal framework, identifies revenue stage, sets 3 quarterly rocks with weekly milestones. Writes new state to Notion and Drive. Runs /executive-handoff with quarter-specific directives.

📣

AI CMO: Align content strategy to rocks

Sync state, say "CMO mode": content strategy built around Rock 1 or 2. /competitor-scraper run at quarter start to reset differentiation analysis. New 30-day calendar for Month 1.

📈

AI CRO: Set revenue targets and outreach plan

Sync state, run /pipeline-revenue-forecast sets the 90-day revenue target, calculates required pipeline volume, identifies how many Apollo leads and conversations needed per week.

⚙

AI COO: Capacity planning for the quarter

Sync state, say "COO mode": checks whether team can deliver the quarterly rocks. /team-performance-pulse runs to establish baseline. SOPs prioritised for quarter. Delegation decisions made before work starts.

💸

AI CFO: Financial plan for the quarter

Sync state, run /revenue-dashboard-agent and /offer-profitability-scanner ensures pricing supports the revenue target set by CRO. Flags any financial constraints affecting the 3 rocks.

10

Pipeline to Revenue Forecast

Connect real pipeline data with the CFO financial model to forecast the next 90 days. Know whether your pipeline is sufficient to hit your financial targets before the month starts.

📈

AI CRO: Run /pipeline-revenue-forecast

Reads active deals from Notion and Gmail. Builds a deal-by-deal probability matrix. Forecasts Month 1 (likely closes), Month 2 (mid-pipeline), Month 3 (top-of-funnel to build now). Cross-references close rate history.

🚨

Flags raised automatically

Month 1 below break-even = RED FLAG. Month 2 pipeline thin = AMBER. Month 3 pipeline empty = flag to CEO Brain and CMO for campaign planning. Each flag includes the specific corrective action.

💸

AI CFO: Validate the forecast against financial model

Forecast numbers fed into CFO cash flow model. If Month 2 forecast is below break-even, CFO checks whether current cash runway bridges the gap or whether immediate revenue action is needed.

🧠

CEO Brain: Campaign runner activated if pipeline thin

If Month 3 pipeline is empty, CEO Brain runs /campaign-runner automatically to brief CMO and CRO on a lead generation campaign designed to fill the Month 3 gap.
